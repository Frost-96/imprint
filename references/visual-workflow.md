# Visual direction, concepts, construction, and iteration

Read when developing the visual direction, concepts, monochrome logo, refinements, and color system. The main skill governs strategy, selection, quality checks, and resource limits. Use [product marks](product-marks.md) for application and [asset production](asset-production.md) for source and rendering details.

## Visual Direction

Translate the selected name and strategy into a concise direction:

- Visual language and personality: how the traits become visible, and which neighboring impressions would be wrong.
- Geometry and symbol style: contour, curve/corner character, dominant relationships, visual weight, and abstraction or subject approach.
- Composition and complexity: primary use, silhouette ambition, focus, spacing, negative space, and tolerance for detail.
- Typography: relevant lettering character, case, weight, rhythm, and relationship to the symbol.
- Color intention: roles and emotional direction derived from strategy, held as provisional until monochrome construction is sound.
- Anti-patterns: specific category shortcuts or treatments that would undermine this brand.

Purple gradients, glow, glass, sparkles, brains, robots, chat bubbles, infinity loops, hexagons, network nodes, and letters in tiles deserve scrutiny when they are interchangeable technology shorthand. They are not a universal blacklist. Ask whether a feature serves this brand concept; monochrome construction still excludes effects at its initial stage regardless of their possible later value.

Keep the direction concrete enough for another designer to sketch, while leaving space for strategically different concepts. Record fixed requirements separately from hypotheses.

## Concept exploration and critique

Explore a few different interpretations of product behavior, experience, relationships, or personality. For each state:

- The core metaphor or idea and why it belongs to the product.
- How the symbol or wordmark would form and which visible feature would carry the idea.
- The traits it expresses and the source of differentiation.
- How it could extend into a visual system without requiring extra symbolism now.
- Its principal risk, including explanation dependence or familiar category imagery.

When the subject is open or figurative, read [figurative marks](figurative-marks.md). Use [character marks](character-marks.md) only for a character direction. Subject choice, construction, and style are separate decisions; changing color or surface finish does not make a new concept.

Before detailed construction, compare the hypotheses and inexpensive monochrome studies against product fit, visible character, distinctiveness, and plausible extension. Resolve a weak central idea before advancing the selected direction. Text-only hypotheses can be reviewed as concepts, with silhouette and execution claims explicitly unverified. Resolve missing visual evidence through sketches before recommending a direction on visual merit. A convincing story cannot establish distinctiveness in an unseen mark.

Compare the selected direction with its strongest viable alternative using the existing sketches. Record one visible advantage and one cost of the selection; a new rationale for the same form does not establish superiority. Keep this in the compact working record rather than adding a separate approval stage.

## Monochrome construction

Work in black and white. Exclude gradients, glow, shadows, textures, glass, 3D, and decorative lighting until the mark's structure is sound. Antialiasing in a render is not a decorative tone or effect.

Build whole to detail: silhouette → proportions and mass → defining relationships and negative spaces → curves, joins, spacing, and optical corrections. A recognizable subject is insufficient without a particular contour, gesture, or relationship that gives this brand identity. Simplicity means clarity and controlled hierarchy, not the fewest possible shapes.

Inspect both positive and reversed structure. For wordmarks, check letter recognition, spacing rhythm, weight, and characteristic letter relationships. Optical balance can require departures from mathematical equality. Avoid arbitrary numerical geometry that lacks a visible purpose.

Use image generation for new raster concepts through the available imagegen tool/skill; use vector editing and rendered previews for vector construction. An approved raster concept still needs faithful editable vector production for the core logo. Investigate feasibility early without selecting only whatever is easiest to draw.

Generate separate candidate assets and preserve original output. Assemble comparisons afterward. Record candidate ID/version, concept, file path, actual dimensions, exact submitted prompt, input/output distinction, and known tool/provider information. A prompt is not a draft, and a generation record is not a reproducibility guarantee.

## Visual inspection and controlled redesign

Inspect actual current renders at large and intended use sizes. Evaluate:

- Macro: visible concept, silhouette, distinctive feature, memory potential, composition, visual language, character, category differentiation, and system potential.
- Micro: curves, joins, edges, alignment, proportions, spacing, negative spaces, visual center, weight, optical correction, and reduction.

Inspect the artwork before writing its final rationale. Verify any claimed hole, fold, connection, gesture, or secondary reading. If absent, correct the work or withdraw the claim; prose cannot fulfill a fixed visual requirement.

Identify whether the failure is concept, character, form, or reproduction. Use the main skill's iteration priority: return concept failures to concept exploration, structural failures to monochrome construction, and reduction failures to application-specific adaptation. Make controlled variants addressing concrete findings while preserving successful features. Compare before/after renders for drift and verify that the change addresses the observed defect. Do not use new effects to hide unresolved structure.

## Color and finish

After monochrome review and iteration, derive the color system from strategy: primary, supporting, neutral, and background roles with explicit values, monochrome and reversed versions. Verify contrast and identity on intended backgrounds through actual renders. Keep the palette as small as its roles permit; supporting roles need not imply extra decorative colors.

Material, lighting, depth, or texture may then enrich an appropriate application. Keep a viable unadorned core. Inspect the complete logo and applicable wordmark/lockup, color, monochrome, reversed, and placement views. Sound monochrome construction does not establish the quality of the finished treatment. Route materially changed geometry back through monochrome review. An attractive mockup is not evidence of a sound mark or a verified export.

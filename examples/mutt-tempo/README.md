## Positioning and naming

Scheduled neighborhood walks for busy dog owners, emphasizing a stable rhythm and each dog’s personality.

Positioning: **Bring daily steps back to a pace that suits the dog.**

Personality: active, through a forward-leaning body; playful, through asymmetric ears; reliable, through solid and clear lettering.

Avoid: veterinary crosses, exaggerated fangs, and generic paw prints.

Naming territories: footsteps and companionship, risking musical associations; an imperfect dog, risking an overly childish tone; a familiar route, risking map-tool associations.

Selected: **Mutt Tempo** (mut TEM-poh), connecting a dog’s character with its everyday pace.

| Candidate | Strengths / intention | Cost / risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mutt Tempo | Connects a dog’s personality and daily pace. | Not every owner likes the colloquial tone of “mutt.” |
| Heel Company | A double meaning of walking and company. | Could be mistaken for footwear. |
| Around Again | Familiarity in a repeated route. | A common phrase. |

## Concepts and refinement

![Three drawn monochrome directions](exploration/concepts.png)

| Direction | Visual relationship | Main risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A / One happy stride | A long body, leaning head, and raised foot show a step. | Legs may merge at small sizes. |
| B / A loose lead | A curved lead outlines an open m. | Could resemble noodles or a generic loop. |
| C / Uneven ears | One upright and one drooping ear above a broad muzzle. | A common front-facing pet-brand portrait. |

Selected **A**: the side-on stride turns everyday pace into an action, fitting dog walking better than a frontal pet portrait. The legs are more complex at small sizes.

Refinement: Increased the gaps between the main legs, adjusted the forward foot, and opened a counter for the ear. Removed ear and eye details in the 32/16 px versions.

## Usage and files

- Primary ink `#472B1C`; light background `#FFE6AE`; accent `#EE9A3D`; supporting color `#B79569`. Use the dark version on light backgrounds and the reversed version on dark backgrounds.

- Wordmark: DynaPuff / RangePuddlefolk. This uses licensed type, spacing, and outlined glyphs; it is not an original typeface. Font files, OFL licenses, and provenance are in [fonts](fonts/). Install the bundled fonts before editing the text version; prefer outlined artwork for general use.

- Recommended full-logo canvas width: **200 px** or more. Standalone symbol: **32 px** or more. Use the symbol from 32 px. The 16 px sample is diagnostic and does not retain facial detail.

- Sizes refer to canvas width; keep the aspect ratio. Preserve the SVG’s built-in whitespace and leave at least 1/8 of the symbol width outside it. For a standalone wordmark, leave at least half the capital-letter height outside it. Do not stretch, add shadows or outlines, or squeeze fine lettering into an avatar. Use white or light reversed artwork on dark backgrounds.

- `sources/`: color, black, and white logo compositions, outlined wordmarks and symbols, an editable text wordmark, and a small-size symbol.
- `exports/`: transparent PNGs. A 16 px export is a diagnostic sample; use it only where the recommendations above support that size.
- `previews/`: color and reversed presentations and avatar studies, not a platform submission icon package.
- `verification/`: actual-size images, source mappings, and rendering/export consistency results.

## Checks and limitations

Checked the three drawn monochrome directions, refinement, large-scale structure, 1024 / 256 / 64 / 32 / 16 px symbols, the full logo at 200 px wide, and final light/dark presentations. Text and outlined versions were compared at 2400 px width, with a contour-overlap IoU of 1.00000000.

The SVGs render without embedded bitmaps. Main assets have no font dependencies except the designated editable text version.

NOTCH — NAME + LOGO

A fictional field-recording catalog for sound designers.
Name: Notch (one syllable). Logo styling: NOTCH.

USING THE LOGO
Use exports/logo-color.svg on light surfaces, logo-black.svg for single-ink
artwork, and logo-white.svg on dark backgrounds. PNG versions are 2048 × 522 px
with transparent backgrounds.

Preserve the SVG's proportions and canvas margins. The viewBox is
-14 -14 424 108 and includes at least 14 source units of clear space.
Use the complete logo at a canvas width of at least 160 px. Do not crop,
stretch, rearrange the strips, close the notches, or replace the lettering.

EDITING
source/master.svg contains editable vector paths and requires no installed font.
source/text-editable.svg keeps the lettering as live text. Install the bundled
RangeTerminal font before editing it. RangeTerminal is a renamed static
JetBrains Mono 500 instance. RangeBody, derived from Manrope 500, is used in
the preview typography. Font sources and license notices are in fonts/.

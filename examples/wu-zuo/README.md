## Positioning and naming

Small batches of freshly cooked meals for nearby workers and residents: a place to sit down for a proper lunch.

Positioning: **Make room to sit down for the midday meal.**

**午坐** (suggested pronunciation: wǔ zuò) refers to sitting down to eat at noon. It connects seating and shared space; no origin in a particular poem is claimed.

Personality: approachable, through a Chinese name and readable lettering; orderly, through controlled stroke weights rather than a nostalgic grocery-store look; everyday warmth, through the spatial relationship between a bowl and narrow lane walls.

Avoid: trendy flame symbols, imitation calligraphy, and the distance of fine dining.

Naming territories: everyday moments — 午巷 / 半晌食; a familiar seat — 邻座饭 / 同桌里; household objects — 碗边 / 一勺正好; replacement after screening — 午坐.

| Candidate | Rationale / strengths | Cost / risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 午坐 | Sitting down for lunch; seating and shared space, without a claimed poetic origin. | Screening found the restaurant 辰午巷, prompting the move away from 午巷. 午坐 preserves the midday pause but needs spoken-spelling tests against 无座. |
| 午巷 | Short, with a clear Chinese visual association. | Needs a food-category descriptor. |
| 半晌食 | Conversational and related to eating. | Pronunciation and interpretation vary regionally. |
| 邻座饭 | Clearly suggests eating together. | More descriptive of the service. |

The similar restaurant name 辰午巷 prompted the change from 午巷. 午坐 retains the intended midday pause; confusion with 无座 remains to be tested.

## Concepts and refinement

![Three drawn monochrome directions](exploration/concepts.png)

| Direction | Visual relationship | Main risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A / A bowl between walls | A bowl sits between two lane walls; the rim and entrance share an opening. | Thin verticals can merge when reduced. |
| B / Noon over the counter | A midday semicircle above a long counter. | Resembles a sunrise hospitality brand, with a weaker food connection. |
| C / A place at the table | Four seats surround an open center. | Resembles a furniture or community-space identity. |

Selected **A**: the boundaries and bowl describe a seated meal, more directly than the sunrise and with a more tangible object than the seating grid.

Refinement: Thickened the lane walls to bring their weight closer to the bowl. The enclosing spatial relationship stayed after the name changed to 午坐.

## Usage and files

- Primary ink `#752C22`; light background `#FFF0CE`; accent `#CC4935`; supporting color `#E7B587`. Accents are for blocks or decoration and do not replace text-contrast checks.

- Wordmark: ZCOOL XiaoWei. This uses licensed type, spacing, and outlined glyphs; it is not an original typeface. Font files, OFL licenses, and provenance are in [fonts](fonts/). Install the bundled fonts before editing the text version; prefer outlined artwork for general use.

- Recommended full-logo canvas width: **140 px** or more. Standalone symbol: **16 px** or more. Use the full Chinese wordmark on signage and menus. Small avatars use only the bowl and boundaries; do not force the characters into a tiny icon.

- Sizes refer to canvas width; keep the aspect ratio. Preserve the SVG’s built-in whitespace and leave at least 1/8 of the symbol width outside it. Use `mark-small.svg` at 32/16 px. Do not stretch, add shadows or outlines, or squeeze fine lettering into an avatar. Use white or light reversed artwork on dark backgrounds.

- `sources/`: color, black, and white logo compositions, outlined wordmarks and symbols, an editable text wordmark, and a small-size symbol.
- `exports/`: transparent PNGs. A 16 px export is a diagnostic sample; use it only where the recommendations above support that size.
- `previews/`: color and reversed presentations and avatar studies, not a platform submission icon package.
- `verification/`: actual-size images, source mappings, and rendering/export consistency results.

## Checks and limitations

Checked the three drawn monochrome directions, refinement, large-scale structure, 256 / 64 / 32 / 16 px symbols, the full logo at 140 px wide, and final light/dark presentations. Text and outlined versions were compared at 2400 px width, with a contour-overlap IoU of 0.99999731.

All 11 SVGs render without embedded bitmaps and have no font dependencies except the designated text version. All 23 final PNGs are byte-for-byte identical to fresh renders of the final SVGs. Passing file checks does not mean every tested size is suitable for use.

Name screening (2026-10-02): The original name 午巷 returned directory leads for the restaurant 辰午巷. The similar name in the same category prompted a change. Searches for 午坐 returned poetry-title pages; these do not establish an etymology or availability for a food business.

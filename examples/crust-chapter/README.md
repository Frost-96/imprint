## Positioning and naming

Small batches of everyday fermented bread for neighbors’ breakfasts and dinners to take home.

Positioning: **Take time today over tomorrow morning’s first bite.**

**Crust Chapter** (krust CHAP-ter) treats the daily slice as a chapter of everyday life. “Chapter” is a brand metaphor, not a publication claim.

Personality: substantial and warm, through a broad base and heavy serifs; rhythmic, through one main score; everyday, through malt yellow and dark baked tones.

Avoid: invented health benefits or fermentation durations, French crowns, and wheat ears everywhere.

Naming territories: preparing tomorrow — Morrow Grain / Early Keeping; everyday life in slices — Crust Chapter / Slice Company; time by the oven — Warm Interval / Oven Pause; selected replacement after screening — Crust Chapter.

| Candidate | Rationale / strengths | Cost / risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Crust Chapter | A daily slice as a chapter of life, used as a metaphor. | An existing Morrow & Grain bakery template prompted the rename. The replacement keeps the everyday baking idea; two lines accommodate its length. |
| Morrow Grain | A natural connection between time and material. | “Morrow” feels literary. |
| Crust Chapter | The image of a daily slice. | Literary, and potentially harder to extend. |
| Warm Interval | Gentle and rhythmic. | Does not clearly indicate food. |

The existing Morrow & Grain bakery template on Framer prompted the change. Crust Chapter preserves the everyday baking intention with a two-line wordmark.

## Concepts and refinement

![Three drawn monochrome directions](exploration/concepts.png)

| Direction | Visual relationship | Main risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A / Tomorrow in the loaf | An arched loaf is opened by one rising score. | Shares category similarities with generic bread imagery. |
| B / Two hands, one grain | Two supporting curves surround a seed. | Could suggest plant care or an environmental brand. |
| C / A morning window | A line of light sits above a flat-topped oven opening. | More architectural than A. |

Selected **A**: an arched loaf is closer to a neighborhood bakery than hands holding a seed. An everyday object gives it substance without a crown.

Refinement: The first two leaf-shaped scores looked like eyes. Replaced them with one rising horizontal score, keeping a stable baked base.

## Usage and files

- Primary ink `#50372E`; light background `#F4D67F`; accent `#B36435`; supporting color `#F7EBC9`. Accents are for blocks or decoration and do not replace text-contrast checks.

- Wordmark: WallFraunces, a fixed-axis instance of Fraunces; CHAPTER uses Manrope. This uses licensed type, spacing, and outlined glyphs; it is not an original typeface. Font files, OFL licenses, and provenance are in [fonts](fonts/). Install the bundled fonts before editing the text version; prefer outlined artwork for general use.

- Recommended full-logo canvas width: **230 px** or more. Standalone symbol: **16 px** or more. The small version widens the score. Do not force the two-line wordmark into 16 px.

- Sizes refer to canvas width; keep the aspect ratio. Preserve the SVG’s built-in whitespace and leave at least 1/8 of the symbol width outside it. Use `mark-small.svg` at 32/16 px. Do not stretch, add shadows or outlines, or squeeze fine lettering into an avatar. Use white or light reversed artwork on dark backgrounds.

- `sources/`: color, black, and white logo compositions, outlined wordmarks and symbols, an editable text wordmark, and a small-size symbol.
- `exports/`: transparent PNGs. A 16 px export is a diagnostic sample; use it only where the recommendations above support that size.
- `previews/`: color and reversed presentations and avatar studies, not a platform submission icon package.
- `verification/`: actual-size images, source mappings, and rendering/export consistency results.

## Checks and limitations

Checked the three drawn monochrome directions, refinement, large-scale structure, 256 / 64 / 32 / 16 px symbols, the full logo at 230 px wide, and final light/dark presentations. Text and outlined versions were compared at 2400 px width, with a contour-overlap IoU of 1.00000000.

All 11 SVGs render without embedded bitmaps and have no font dependencies except the designated text version. All 23 final PNGs are byte-for-byte identical to fresh renders of the final SVGs. Passing file checks does not mean every tested size is suitable for use.

Name screening (2026-10-02): The Framer marketplace already contained a Morrow & Grain bakery template in the same category, so Morrow Grain was abandoned. Follow-up searches did not establish a verified bread brand called Crust Chapter.

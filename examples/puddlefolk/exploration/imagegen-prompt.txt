Center a single entire mark with generous clear space, approximately 65 percent of canvas width. This is a brand mark not a scene, no puddle, no leaf, no surrounding objects, no gradients, no shading, no texture, no 3D, no frame, no drop shadow, no mockup, no text. Do not imitate an existing character. Aim for sophisticated playful illustration that can be faithfully reconstructed as a small set of closed vector paths and still read at 32 pixels.

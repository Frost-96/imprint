## Positioning and naming

Recombinable wooden parts for families who enjoy hands-on assembly, supporting children and adults playing together.

Positioning: **Turn a table into a small workshop where you can start again.**

Personality: mischievous, through offset forms without disorder; buildable, through blocks and a round axle; orderly, through a clear typographic hierarchy.

Avoid: baby bottles, claims of educational outcomes, and rainbow gradients.

Naming territories: play against the seriousness of a bureau, risking adult-office associations; the sound of parts, risking baby-rattle associations; unstable balance, risking impressions of poor quality.

Selected: **Rattle Bureau** (RAT-uhl BYUR-oh), contrasting the sound of parts with a straight-faced office.

| Candidate | Strengths / intention | Cost / risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rattle Bureau | The sound of parts against a serious-sounding bureau. | “Rattle” may suggest an infant’s toy. |
| Odd Assembly | Allows unusual combinations. | Too close to Swerve Assembly within this collection. |
| Stackabout | A short coined word about stacking play. | Likely spelling variations. |

## Concepts and refinement

![Three drawn monochrome directions](exploration/concepts.png)

| Direction | Visual relationship | Main risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A / A modular R | A round hole, square stem, and slanted leg form an R. | A relatively ordinary single-letter structure. |
| B / The wobbly assembly | A tilted beam, round axle, and two offset bases. | Suggests a balancing toy; joints must not merge. |
| C / Four possible pieces | Four notched modules surround an open center. | Could become a puzzle icon. |

Selected **B**: the tilted beam, axle, and bases form a recombinable toy relationship, with more unstable playfulness than a letter or puzzle.

Refinement: Separated the previously touching beam, axle, and bases, reduced the central circle, and widened the gap between the bases.

## Usage and files

- Primary ink `#124C3D`; light background `#F8E8D2`; accent `#F2A9BF`; supporting color `#9DBF93`. Use the dark version on light backgrounds and the reversed version on dark backgrounds.

- Wordmark: Manrope / RangeBodyBold + RangeBody. This uses licensed type, spacing, and outlined glyphs; it is not an original typeface. Font files, OFL licenses, and provenance are in [fonts](fonts/). Install the bundled fonts before editing the text version; prefer outlined artwork for general use.

- Recommended full-logo canvas width: **210 px** or more. Standalone symbol: **32 px** or more. Use the symbol from 32 px. The beam-to-circle gap is weak at 16 px, so that export is diagnostic only.

- Sizes refer to canvas width; keep the aspect ratio. Preserve the SVG’s built-in whitespace and leave at least 1/8 of the symbol width outside it. For a standalone wordmark, leave at least half the capital-letter height outside it. Do not stretch, add shadows or outlines, or squeeze fine lettering into an avatar. Use white or light reversed artwork on dark backgrounds.

- `sources/`: color, black, and white logo compositions, outlined wordmarks and symbols, an editable text wordmark, and a small-size symbol.
- `exports/`: transparent PNGs. A 16 px export is a diagnostic sample; use it only where the recommendations above support that size.
- `previews/`: color and reversed presentations and avatar studies, not a platform submission icon package.
- `verification/`: actual-size images, source mappings, and rendering/export consistency results.

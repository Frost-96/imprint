## Positioning and naming

Small-scale coastal accommodation and a reading space for adult travelers who want to stay slowly for a few days.

Positioning: **Give a quiet stretch of seaside time a shape.**

**Nacre Hour** (suggested reading for this study: NAY-ker hour) combines the material feel of mother-of-pearl with the pause of an hour. A slow coastal stay is the design intention.

Personality: light, through slender letters and generous counters; private, through a partially enclosing shell; gentle, through pale violet and cream.

Avoid: palm-and-sunset templates, healing claims, and ornate wedding-invitation styling.

Naming territories: coastal materials — Nacre Hour / Shell Interlude; a pace of staying — Stay Slowly / Unhurried Rooms; light at the edge — Pale Inlet / Window Tide.

| Candidate | Rationale / strengths | Cost / risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nacre Hour | A delicate pairing of material and time. | “Nacre” needs pronunciation and spelling guidance. |
| Pale Inlet | A clear coastal space. | Could feel cold. |
| Stay Slowly | Friendly tone. | More like a tagline. |

## Concepts and refinement

![Three drawn monochrome directions](exploration/concepts.png)

| Direction | Visual relationship | Main risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A / A shell at rest | Shell ribs gather at a short base, leaving one side open. | Shells are common coastal-category imagery. |
| B / An unhurried horizon | A crescent and low horizontal line suggest a pause. | Could become a generic moon symbol. |
| C / NH ligature | N and H share a stem and crossbar. | A letter combination feels less approachable. |

Selected **A**: the shell connects to the coastal material in the name. Its open base feels lighter than a closed badge, though the common shell motif retains a category-similarity risk.

Refinement: Kept four ribs at large sizes and reduced them to two thicker ribs in the small version. The high-contrast serif wordmark is not claimed to work at 16 px.

## Usage and files

- Primary ink `#524162`; light background `#F5EFDF`; accent `#C5AFD4`; supporting color `#D8B797`. Accents are for blocks or decoration and do not replace text-contrast checks.

- Wordmark: Bodoni Moda (bundled as RangeLongAside). This uses licensed type, spacing, and outlined glyphs; it is not an original typeface. Font files, OFL licenses, and provenance are in [fonts](fonts/). Install the bundled fonts before editing the text version; prefer outlined artwork for general use.

- Recommended full-logo canvas width: **240 px** or more. Standalone symbol: **32 px** or more. Use the symbol at 32 px or above. The 16 px export is diagnostic, without a promise that all shell ribs remain distinguishable.

- Sizes refer to canvas width; keep the aspect ratio. Preserve the SVG’s built-in whitespace and leave at least 1/8 of the symbol width outside it. Use `mark-small.svg` at 32/16 px. Do not stretch, add shadows or outlines, or squeeze fine lettering into an avatar. Use white or light reversed artwork on dark backgrounds.

- `sources/`: color, black, and white logo compositions, outlined wordmarks and symbols, an editable text wordmark, and a small-size symbol.
- `exports/`: transparent PNGs. A 16 px export is a diagnostic sample; use it only where the recommendations above support that size.
- `previews/`: color and reversed presentations and avatar studies, not a platform submission icon package.
- `verification/`: actual-size images, source mappings, and rendering/export consistency results.

## Checks and limitations

Checked the three drawn monochrome directions, refinement, large-scale structure, 256 / 64 / 32 / 16 px symbols, the full logo at 240 px wide, and final light/dark presentations. Text and outlined versions were compared at 2400 px width, with a contour-overlap IoU of 1.00000000.

All 11 SVGs render without embedded bitmaps and have no font dependencies except the designated text version. All 23 final PNGs are byte-for-byte identical to fresh renders of the final SVGs. Passing file checks does not mean every tested size is suitable for use.

Name screening (2026-10-02): Results included watches and other material containing “nacre” and “hour”; no same-name accommodation was verified. Low-relevance results do not establish registrability.

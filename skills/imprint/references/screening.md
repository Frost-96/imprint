# Evidence and preliminary conflict screening

Use during naming, before investing in a selected visual concept's finishing, and when a review raises a factual conflict. Brand design judgment and real-world availability are separate questions.

Use available search/browsing and applicable research instructions. Record actual queries, date, source links, relevant findings, interpretations, and unchecked scope for each candidate. Prefer primary product, organization, linguistic, or registry sources for factual claims. Inspect compared images before asserting visual similarity.

## Names

Search each credible candidate separately for exact spelling, then with the product category, relevant close spellings/pronunciations, and consequential meaning concerns in priority languages. Explain why spelling or sound variants matter. A multi-name query does not screen each name; spacing changes alone do not cover phonetic confusion.

Assess same and adjacent categories, audience overlap, prominence of relevant uses, and likely search/spoken confusion. Aim for acceptable confusion risk, not zero prior use. Retire clear material conflicts; defer unresolved potentially serious ones. A few remote or limited uses can be acceptable with evidence and a stated rationale. Personal unfamiliarity and sparse results are not evidence of obscurity.

Screen replacements on quality as well as conflict. Deepen checks on the selected name. Only claim checks actually performed: domains, app stores, social handles, and trademark registers require their own searches when requested or necessary to the agreed availability scope. Public name search does not establish those statuses. Registration or legal clearance cannot be inferred from an empty result.

## Visual identities

Search relevant category marks and distinctive motifs using image search or available visual references. Compare actual silhouettes, composition, and motif relationships; distinguish a shared convention from a materially similar overall identity. Record what was inspected. An unverified resemblance concern is a lead for investigation, not verified copying or legal conflict.

## Reporting

Use explicit statuses: not checked, partially checked, preliminary screening completed with stated findings, or unresolved concern. State scope and remaining uncertainty; avoid an unqualified “cleared” label. A discovered conflict with a fixed user choice calls for explanation and an authorized decision, not silent replacement.

Preliminary screening is not trademark clearance, exclusivity, originality proof, native-speaker approval, or audience testing. Access failures leave a check incomplete. Proceed provisionally where appropriate, preserving that status in the final handoff.

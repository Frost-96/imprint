# Product icon design

Read for mobile or desktop application identities, with or without an existing logo. Apply the main skill's [scope and budget](../../SKILL.md#scope-and-operating-rules), [choices](../../SKILL.md#user-choices), and [completion criteria](../../SKILL.md#final-brand-handoff). UI icon sets, menu-bar/tray symbols, and store artwork are separate deliverables unless requested.

## Scope, brief, and choices

Distinguish style trials, finished artwork, and native platform packaging. Extend the shared brief with app behavior, target platforms/surfaces, and existing brand language. Read [platform execution](platforms.md) before masks, layering, or source requirements can invalidate a concept. Trials without a target use labelled assumptions and leave platform readiness unconfirmed.

Read [style choices and design language](direction.md) before concept rendering, then only the relevant construction guides. Record design-language decisions, platform requirements/source dates, and checks alongside the shared candidate record.

Sequence: direction → concepts → concept choice → structure/rendering → platform/context checks → final choice → handoff. For trials, render and inspect meaningful alternatives with launcher-mask and small-size views, differences, and limitations. Keep trial assets in task output unless permanent curation is requested.

## Metaphors and concept territories

Ask what object, character, relationship, or behavior this software could become. Tools, spatial relationships, product behavior, letters, and abstraction are possible sources, not a checklist. A rendering treatment alone is not a metaphor; an indirect metaphor need not explain every feature.

Record each concept's product relationship, dominant form, identifying feature, brand connection, platform/reduction potential, and principal risk. Different finishes on one symbol are one concept family. Familiar category objects need a particular construction or relationship; initials alone do not establish identity.

Draw economical separate concept assets using silhouette, color masses, or rough depth as needed. Inspect for fit, distinctiveness, explanation dependence, structure, reduction, and longevity. Present viable candidates at comparable finish/size with the recommendation's visible advantage and trade-off; obtain the concept choice before costly rendering. Use [visual screening](../screening.md) within scope before finishing.

## Structure before finish

Resolve dominant object, silhouette, proportion, optical center, orientation, negative space, foreground/background, edges, visual weight, and depth hierarchy. Organize primary form → secondary form → supporting detail → micro detail. Inspect reduced-detail/color-mass studies: recognition must survive removal of micro detail, but may depend on color/depth rather than monochrome-logo viability.

Apply the chosen [construction guides](direction.md#design-language-and-reference-routing), combining subject and rendering guidance. Character construction applies across flat, painted, dimensional, and pixel styles. Finish should clarify the concept and personality; ease of coding is not a concept-selection criterion.

Use available image-generation tools and their instructions for raster artwork, and vector construction for controlled geometry or exact typography. Generate standalone masters, keeping device scenes and comparison boards separate. Preserve originals and prompts through the shared decision record; source/editability requirements follow [asset production](../asset-production.md).

### Render brief

Build a prompt from actual decisions, omitting inapplicable fields:

> Application identity for [behavior/user] on [platform or provisional context]. Direction: [treatment/reference cues]. Subject: [metaphor and identifying relationship]. Composition: [masses, viewpoint, crop, protected features, negative space]. Reduction: [features that survive]. Language: [shape, color roles, edges, shading, texture; material/light when relevant]. Output: [standalone master/layers and background]. Edits: [target, preserved invariants, problem to change]. Keep presentation text, device scenes, and display margins outside the master.

Inspect continuous edges, asymmetry, openings, expression, and color boundaries. Dimensional work also needs coherent material response, lighting, contact, perspective, and reflections. Correct melted geometry, fake text, stray marks, and background artifacts while preserving intentional stylization. Reassess style fidelity, composition, and identity after cleanup.

## Platform, size, and environment evidence

Follow [platform execution](platforms.md) per target and [asset verification](../asset-production.md#verify-actual-files) for reduction studies. Matching platform executions are valid when separately verified; a universal PNG alone does not establish readiness.

Create relevant home-screen, Dock/Finder, launcher, or Start/taskbar/search previews at documented displayed dimensions, with contrasting backgrounds/appearances where applicable. Use representative neighbors with comparable visual weight and varied finish/complexity; uniformly simple glyphs cannot establish presence among mature icons. Use supplied or legitimately available references, or labelled synthetic comparators. Record provenance and missing coverage; narrow conclusions for unrepresentative comparisons. Schematics are not device tests or measured discoverability.

Inspect masks, visual mass, openings, hierarchy, detail noise, depth, optical center, and presence among neighbors. Use reduction/blur studies to test dominant shape, color, and composition. An interchangeable tile/blob calls for structural or concept revision. Compare isolated masters with placements; device shadows, wallpaper casts, and surrounding UI belong only in previews.

## Final evaluation and iteration

Inspect the exact proposed delivery without its design story:

| Layer | Questions and failure destination |
| --- | --- |
| Identity | Plausible product relationship, memorable feature, distinctiveness, shared brand DNA? Failures → strategy/concepts. |
| Icon | Effective silhouette, hierarchy, color identity, reduction, context presence, and target executions? Failures → structure/platform adaptation. |
| Craft | Coherent geometry, material, light, perspective, edges, typography, and color? Failures → rendering. |

Prioritize concept → silhouette/composition → brand relationship → required-use performance → material/color → micro detail. An attractive illustration does not establish a functioning icon; elaborate craft cannot compensate for a failed required use. Follow the shared iteration budget and family check, repeating affected checks after changes.

## Final choice and handoff

Prepare checked files through [asset production](../asset-production.md). Present the exact finished standalone icon, realistic target-context preview, small-size evidence, and meaningful trade-offs or limitations. Keep artwork readiness and native-package validation separate, then apply shared selection and completion rules.

# Wordmarks and lettermarks

Read for logos made from a complete name, letters/initials, or a symbol with a wordmark. Apply the chosen [logo direction](direction.md), [monochrome construction](workflow.md#monochrome-construction), and [asset production](../asset-production.md). Preserve the selected spelling, script, and approved capitalization; renaming is a separate decision.

## Establish the lettering idea

Use the actual name to explore how letterforms, rhythm, and a characteristic relationship can express the brand. Compare a few meaningful approaches within the chosen direction: a different letter structure, proportion, rhythm, or custom gesture. Swapping similar fonts without changing visible character is a weak concept comparison. A wordmark can be the complete logo; standalone symbols and monograms are additional choices only when requested or selected.

Choose a documented typeface as a foundation when its construction fits, or develop custom lettering when the desired identity needs it. Determine source feasibility early and follow the production reference's font licensing and editability requirements. Raster generation can explore a treatment, but reconstruct the exact letters with controlled geometry or a real font before claiming a finished wordmark.

## Build and refine a wordmark

- **Recognition:** Check every glyph in the actual name. Preserve enough structure to distinguish similar letters, counters, joins, and relevant script features; a clever alteration that repeatedly changes the reading needs revision.
- **Overall rhythm:** Resolve the word's silhouette, width, weight, density, and baseline behavior before individual flourishes. Compare it in intended placements so a long name is not judged only as a large isolated image.
- **Spacing:** Work from the visible spaces between shapes, not uniform numeric gaps. Check difficult pairs, round/straight combinations, punctuation, and start/end balance. Reinspect the whole word after adjusting a pair.
- **Distinctive changes:** Give custom cuts, ligatures, terminals, or shared strokes a visible role. Concentrate identity in a coherent relationship instead of modifying every letter. Ensure ligatures preserve the selected reading and do not collapse when reduced.
- **Optical corrections:** Compare apparent stroke weight, curve overshoot, tight joins, counter size, and positive/reversed behavior. Mathematical alignment is a starting point, not proof of balance.

For Latin lettering, inspect case, x-height/cap relationships, ascenders/descenders, and repeated letters in context. For Chinese lettering, preserve character identity, component relationships, stroke hierarchy, and interior openings; balance different character densities rather than forcing identical stroke counts or blindly imposing Latin case/baseline concepts. For other scripts, use their own joining and shaping requirements and disclose uncertain reading judgments.

For a requested multilingual identity, treat each script as lettering in its own right. Establish their shared tone and relative hierarchy; an equal point size rarely establishes equal apparent weight. Use the selected paired names rather than silently inventing translations to fill a lockup.

## Letters and monograms

Establish the selected letters and intended reading order. Explore overlap, interlock, shared strokes, or negative space only when the relationship is clear enough for the brief. Compare connected and separated studies when a clever combination loses the letters. Inspect accidental alternate readings and generic initial-in-a-shape constructions in context; initials alone do not supply a distinctive concept.

A lettermark has its own silhouette and reduction behavior. Check internal openings, crossings, density, and visual center in monochrome and reversed form. Do not automatically place it in a square or use it as the app icon; those uses have separate composition and validation requirements.

## Lockups, verification, and selection

For requested symbol-and-wordmark combinations, define reading order, apparent scale, alignment, and spacing as a relationship between the two elements. Derive horizontal or stacked variants for actual uses rather than manufacturing every possible arrangement. Use [applications](applications.md) to assign optical variants and supported sizes.

Inspect large renders for curves and joins, then realistic widths/heights for reading, rhythm, and merging counters. Verify exact spelling and correspondence between editable text and outlined sources through asset production. Keep font-based text-editable sources distinct from custom outline artwork. Present the finished lettering and requested lockups through the workflow's final choice step; a selected font or lettering sketch is not acceptance of the final wordmark.

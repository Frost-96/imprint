# Logo design

Read for every logo request, including wordmarks, symbol-only work, refinements, and trials. Apply the main skill's [scope and budget](../../SKILL.md#scope-and-operating-rules), [choices](../../SKILL.md#user-choices), and [completion criteria](../../SKILL.md#final-brand-handoff).

## Scope, brief, and direction

Establish intended components, placements, backgrounds, and media from the shared brief. For naming-plus-logo work, settle the exact name before final lettering; label authorized provisional studies accordingly.

Read [logo directions](direction.md) before sketches to resolve form and visual language and select construction references. Record a provisional color intention; palette exploration follows sound monochrome structure. Consult [asset production](../asset-production.md) early when editable-source feasibility affects concept choice.

Sequence: direction → concepts → concept choice → monochrome construction → color/applications → final choice → handoff. For trials, the deliverable is an inspected comparison at useful large and intended small sizes. Vary treatment for style trials and underlying ideas for concept comparisons; label what changed.

## Concept exploration and critique

Explore distinct concepts through the brand's purpose, experience, relationships, or personality. Record each concept's metaphor, visible identifying feature, brand fit, extension potential, and principal risk. Different stories or finishes yielding the same form are one concept family.

Use [lettering](lettering.md) for wordmarks/lettermarks, [figurative construction](figurative.md) for subjects/characters, and monochrome construction below for abstract marks. Keep subject, composition, and style as separate decisions.

Compare inexpensive actual monochrome studies for fit, visible character, distinctiveness, explanation dependence, and extension. Text-only hypotheses leave visual merit unverified; render sketches before making visual recommendations. Present viable candidates at comparable finish and scale, including the strongest alternative, with a visible advantage and real trade-off. Obtain the concept choice before detailed refinement. Use [visual screening](../screening.md) within scope before investing in finishing.

## Monochrome construction

Work in black and white before decorative color, material, lighting, or effects. Build silhouette → proportions/mass → defining relationships/negative spaces → curves, joins, spacing, and optical corrections. Clarity need not mean the fewest shapes. Inspect positive and reversed structure; mathematical equality is not proof of optical balance.

For abstract marks, test how masses connect, separate, repeat, or turn. The identifying relationship must read without its explanation and survive reduction. Refine intervals and openings rather than construction-grid numerology. Negative space need not conceal a second symbol, and abstract marks need not resemble app tiles.

Use available image-generation tools and their instructions for raster concepts, and vector editing with rendered previews for controlled construction. Preserve separate original candidate assets and assemble comparisons afterward. A raster concept still requires faithful editable-vector production for the core logo; investigate feasibility without choosing only what is easiest to code.

Complete construction when material structural problems are resolved in positive/reversed and intended-size renders.

## Visual inspection and controlled redesign

Inspect current renders before writing the rationale:

- **Identity:** Visible concept, silhouette, distinguishing feature, composition, character, category differentiation, and system potential. Verify claimed holes, folds, connections, gestures, and secondary readings in the artwork.
- **Craft:** Curves, joins, edges, proportions, spacing, negative spaces, visual weight, optical center, and reduction.

Prioritize concept → distinctiveness → silhouette/composition → geometry/character → micro details → color. Return failures to their responsible stage, including the brief when strategy is wrong. Interpret feedback through the underlying design problem. Compare controlled before/after variants for both improvement and drift; repeat affected checks.

## Color and finish

Derive the palette from strategy, with exact primary/supporting/neutral/background values where applicable. Keep it as small as its roles permit. Inspect the finished logo and requested lockups in color, monochrome, reversed, and intended-background placements.

Material, depth, or texture may enrich applications while preserving an unadorned core. Materially changed geometry returns to monochrome review. A mockup does not verify the underlying artwork or exports.

## Final choice and handoff

Use [applications](applications.md) for requested placements and optical variants, and [asset production](../asset-production.md) for checked sources, exports, and usage guidance. Present the finished standalone logo, applicable color/monochrome/reversed versions and lockups, small-size evidence, and a relevant use preview. Identify the exact version and trade-offs, then apply the shared selection and completion rules.
